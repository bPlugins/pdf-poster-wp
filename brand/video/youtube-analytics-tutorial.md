HOW THE COUNTING WORKS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A view is counted once the viewer has actually rendered the document and it has been on screen for a second — then not again for that same visitor for 30 minutes, so a reader flicking between pages counts once.

It works behind a page cache, because counting happens in the visitor's browser rather than while the page is being built.

And it stays on your site: no IP address is stored, nothing is sent to a third party, and the rows live in your own database.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FREE vs PRO
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Free: counting itself, plus today's views and downloads on the list, in the editor panel and on the Analytics screen. Settings to switch counting off, exclude your own visits, or respect Do Not Track.
Pro: the history — 7, 30 and 90 day ranges, change against the previous period, download rate, busiest day, your top documents ranked, all-time totals, sortable columns and CSV export.

Every day is recorded in your own database either way, so there is nothing to import if you upgrade later — the history your site has already built is there waiting.
